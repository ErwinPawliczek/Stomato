# amine_sulfinol_m — Amine package with physical-solvent co-absorption (Sulfolane)

family `electrolyte` · status **provisional** · adapter `ngdesign.amine.package:AmineSulfinolPackage`.

## Applicability (Carlson 1996)

- component classes: water, amine, acid_gas
- polar: True · electrolyte: True
- T window: 313.15–313.15 K · P window: 1000–8e+06 Pa
- phases: V, L

## Parameters

- source: data/thermo_params/amine/sulfinol_macgregor1988.yaml
- provenance: MacGregor 1988 (Univ. of Alberta MSc thesis) modified Deshmukh-Mather model, sulfolane + water lumped as the solvent: extended Debye-Hueckel eq. 6.2 with the Chakravarty (1985) beta's of Table 6.2 (the set the thesis adopts, p. 61), mixed-solvent dielectric eq. 6.3, mixed-solvent equilibrium and Henry constants of Table 6.4 (fitted on the single-gas Tables 5.3-5.5; Table 6.5, refitted on the mixed data, is not used). Constants exist for ONE solvent (20.9/30.5/48.6 wt% MDEA/sulfolane/water, 2.217 mol MDEA per kg water + sulfolane; feeds within 5 % of its sulfolane:water ratio and MDEA molality) at TWO isotherms; the model refuses any T other than 313.15 K and 373.15 K (+/-0.01 K) and never interpolates. The 373.15 K isotherm also refuses today: no cited sulfolane permittivity at 373.15 K was retrievable (eq. 6.3 needs it). The 40 C Henry constant of H2S was raised by hand by the author to fit the high-loading single-gas H2S data (p. 66), so H2S predictions at high loading are not independent of the author's tuning. Interpretation choices where the scanned thesis is silent (its pp. 72-85, incl. nomenclature, are absent from the scan): "K(MDEA prot.)" read as the base constant MDEA + H2O = MDEAH+ + OH- (read as printed, MDEAH+ = MDEA + H+, the model overpredicts the thesis's own fitted tables by 2.6x-105x); A the ln-base Debye-Hueckel slope (3 A_phi) on pure-water density; water activity = its liquid mole fraction; aqueous K_w (Edwards 1978) and K(HS-) (kent_eisenberg); pure sulfolane permittivity 42.2 at 313.15 K (Vahidi & Moshtari 2013, ThermoML); vapour fugacity by the carrier PR for the pure gas. Against the fitted single-gas tables at 40 C the reconstruction runs at mean |ln p| 0.55 (CO2, 30 points, biased high) and 0.29 (H2S, 21 points). No heat of absorption (single isotherms): the liquid enthalpy is the carrier's.

Declared exclusions (not silent skips):

- `dhdT_cp` — no temperature dependence (single isotherms), so there is no dH/dT of the reactive liquid to compare with cp
- `gibbs_duhem` — the reactive liquid's ln_phi carries the speciation equilibrium (f_i == p_eq_i phi_i^V), not a Gibbs-excess composition derivative
- `phase_identification` — same basis: the single-liquid limit replaces the vapour-pressure bracket for the reactive liquid
- `pure_psat_limit` — applicability is the loaded MDEA + sulfolane + water liquid; pure components delegate to the carrier, whose pure-saturation limits are that row's surface, not this one's
- `round_trip` — the constants are single isotherms (MacGregor 1988 Table 6.4); a PH/PS flash must move T off the isotherm, which the model refuses by design (no interpolation between 40 and 100 C)
