Progress

Three progress concepts

A plain bar says nothing about what a simulator is doing. Each of these shows real solve information in the octagon style. Press Run to play one; they also play once as they scroll into view.

Pipeline chain

One octagon per stage of the real run, from thermo to the drawing package. The active stage sends out a pulse, and each finished link snaps its diamond into place.

Stage 4 of 8 · 3.5 s of 5.4 s

Residual descent

For a recycle solve, the honest progress is how far the residual has fallen toward the tolerance, not the iteration count. The bar fills on that log scale. Example Wegstein iterations.

The strike

Octagon cells fill while the solve runs. When it converges, the mantis shrimp winds up and hits the end of the bar, and a shockwave goes out.

PFD and P&ID equipment symbols stay the standard ISO and ISA shapes so drawings read correctly. Only the tags, the chrome and the controls are octagons. Text is set in Chakra Petch throughout, with JetBrains Mono kept for numbers so columns of figures line up. The scroll bars are drawn by the page rather than the browser, so they look the same in Chrome, Safari and Firefox.
